NyaySetu

A Step Towards Accessible Justice

NyaySetu is a project focused on making legal information and assistance more accessible to people. The main idea is to simplify access to legal resources and help users find relevant information through a user-friendly platform.

About the Project

The purpose of NyaySetu is to bridge the gap between people and legal information by providing a digital platform where users can explore legal resources more conveniently.

This project was developed as part of an academic project to explore how technology can be used to address real-world problems.

Objectives

- Make legal information easier to access.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
- Use technology to address challenges related to legal awareness.
- Develop a practical solution to a real-world problem.

Features

- User-friendly interface.
- Organized project structure.
- Digital access to legal resources.
- Scope for future improvements and additional functionality.
